## An introduction to favicons
I learnt a couple things just now. I made this beautiful favicon on Aseprite in 5mins that in no way resembles the roblox smirk face.  
So to upload that png, I knew I needed ftp, and thankfully DigitalOcean stays based and walked me through it like a baby. Though I used my brain and luck to figure out the `-i webkey` part. I just had to do:
```bash
sftp -i webkey root@xxx.xx.xxx.xx
pwd
cd /var/www/riggi.me/html
lpwd
lls
put favicon.png
```

Then I was done. See for yourself. Also it is not meant to resemble me.
