##**Moanin'** *Art Blakey & The Jazz Messengers* (1958)
---
01 - **Moanin'**
Instantly recognisable from the beginning. It's a conversation between piano and band throughout. Every instrument gets their turn to splurt out their soul. Difficult to match the energy and virtuosismo shown by the band in this piece. One of those songs were you can't resist to bop your head. Hard Bop doesn't get harder than this. 5/5
